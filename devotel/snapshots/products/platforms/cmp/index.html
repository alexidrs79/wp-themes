FAQ
Got Questions on Devotel's CMP?
Everything you need to know to get started.
What is a Connectivity Management Platform?
CMP is a cloud-based platform that handles the full eSIM lifecycle — from plan creation and activation to top-up, compliance, and analytics. It enables brands to sell eSIMs globally without building telecom infrastructure or negotiating carrier contracts.
How quickly can we launch?
Enterprises can go live in 14 days with our white-label storefront. API integration typically takes under 30 days. Devotel handles carrier setup, compliance, and infrastructure.
Do we need a technical team to manage CMP?
Not necessarily. Our dashboard is designed for business users in mind. However, API access is available for technical teams who want deeper integration with existing systems.
Can we customise our own prices?
Absolutely. You control all pricing, packages, and promotions. Set different prices by region, season, or customer segment.
What support does Devotel provide?
Every customer gets dedicated onboarding, a customer success manager, and 24/7 technical support. We also provide white-labelled end-user support so your customers get help under your brand.
How reliable is your platform?
99.9% uptime SLA backed by multi-region infrastructure and 24/7 monitoring. We provide access to 200+ carrier profiles across 190+ countries through Tier-1 connectivity partners.
What are the Terms and Conditions for using Devotel's CMP?
